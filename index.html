Hi, I’m Farah!
Currently B.Arch & Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University.
I’m a designer driven by curiosity for materials, fabrication, and the way things come together.
My work and interests center on material exploration, facade tectonics, and interior–exterior finishings. I’m particularly interested in how surfaces, assemblies, and details shape spatial experience. I love working hands-on, prototyping, testing, and translating ideas into physical outcomes.
I’m especially drawn to commercial environments, retail spaces, and projects connected to fashion and mall culture, where materiality and atmosphere play a critical role.
Alongside architecture, I maintain a growing interest in UI/UX design, and I’m excited by opportunities that connect digital interactions with material and spatial thinking.
I’m always open to have a conversation whether it be about architecture, materials or fashion, feel free to reach out!
